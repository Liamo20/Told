Why we exist

Every family has a story.
Most of them disappear.

We started Told because we kept watching it happen. The elder who held the whole family's history in their memory. The grandchild who meant to ask. The window that closed without warning.

"We don't just record a life.
We listen for the soul of it."

What we stand for

one

We believe in the voice

Every person carries an accent, a cadence, a way of pausing before the hard parts. A professional writer knows how to draw that out — and how to hold it on the page so it sounds like them, not like a summary.

two

We believe in the object

A screen dims. A file corrupts. A book endures. When a story is held in a physical volume, passed between generations and left on shelves, it earns the weight it always deserved.

three

We believe in the time

There is a specific and irreversible moment when a story becomes unreachable. We exist to meet families before that moment — not after. The work begins now, while the voice is still clear.

This is not a product.

A printed memoir is not a product. It cannot be reordered. It cannot be refreshed next season. The book you hold in thirty years will be the same book produced today — which is precisely why the care taken at the point of making matters so deeply. We treat every commission as we would a piece of furniture built to last a century.

Professional storytelling is not the same as sitting down with a family video camera. It is not an interview conducted out of obligation at Christmas. The writers at Told are trained to ask the questions that a loving family cannot — the ones that feel too large, too painful, or simply too honest for a Tuesday evening conversation. They listen without agenda. They know when to press gently forward and when to let a silence breathe.

And there is the matter of now. The person you want to capture is here. They are alive in their memory, in their warmth, in the particular way they tell a story that everyone in your family has heard before but would give anything to hear again. That is a temporary state. We don't say this to frighten — we say it because it is true, and because the families who come to us having waited too long say the same thing. Begin while you can. Begin today.

The right time is always now

Begin while the voice
is still clear.
